# Presentation credits

*Same Bird Parts, Different Evidence* is the team's two-minute research presentation. This edition uses the original English narration, timing and animated charts. The opening two diagrams illustrate the group's meme ideas. Schematics stand in for the dataset photographs and prototype crops. The measured scores come from the original image experiment.

The team studied [CUB-200-2011](https://www.vision.caltech.edu/datasets/cub_200_2011/) (Wah, Branson, Welinder, Perona and Belongie, 2011) with [PIP-Net](https://github.com/M-Nauta/PIPNet) implemented in [CaBRNet](https://github.com/aiser-team/cabrnet). The [CUB-200 checkpoint](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18429182) was trained and published by Romain Xu-Darme. Part interactions were computed using the [Shapley–Taylor interaction index](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/sundararajan20a.html).
